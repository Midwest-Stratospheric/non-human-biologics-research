### Observation plan

- **B and C are logged identically, on the same days and times**, so they can be compared directly:
  - Counts: active, sluggish, tun (dormant), dead, eggs / exuviae with eggs
  - Water temperature and room temperature; water clarity (clear / cloudy)
  - Feeding: algae-disk piece size, time added, time leftovers removed
  - Water changes (volume and time)
  - Microscope photo/video reference saved with dish ID and date
- **C only (planned):** pre-launch counts and activity; flight data from payload loggers (altitude, pressure, temperature, UV, radiation); after recovery, time to revival, survival rate, and reproduction over following weeks.
- **A:** routine husbandry logging on Form F1 (counts, water, feeding, subculture events).
