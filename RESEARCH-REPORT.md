### Study groups

One A tank, with two replicate pairs of ground-control (B) and flight (C) dishes.

| Group | Housing | Role | Leaves the lab? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **AERO-554-A** | Large tank (home habitat), one only | Stock / reference population and backup supply. Not the formal control. | Never |
| **AERO-554-B1** | Petri dish | Ground control paired with **C1**. Same handling, transport timing and schedule as C1, but does not fly. | Travels with C1, does not fly |
| **AERO-554-B2** | Petri dish | Ground control paired with **C2**. Same handling, transport timing and schedule as C2, but does not fly. | Travels with C2, does not fly |
| **AERO-554-C1** | Petri dish | Flight dish, paired with **B1**. Will fly to the stratosphere on a future mission. | Yes (planned; has not flown) |
| **AERO-554-C2** | Petri dish | Flight dish, paired with **B2**. Will fly to the stratosphere on a future mission. | Yes (planned; has not flown) |

**Fixed pairing:** C1 ↔ B1, C2 ↔ B2. Each pair starts with the same water, temperature, feeding and starting count, so the only planned difference is the flight. Two replicate pairs help show whether any difference repeats or comes from a single dish, and give a backup if a dish is lost.

### Observation plan

- **Each B dish and its paired C dish (B1/C1, B2/C2) are logged identically, on the same days and times**, so they can be compared directly:
  - Counts: active, sluggish, tun (dormant), dead, eggs / exuviae with eggs
  - Water temperature and room temperature; water clarity (clear / cloudy)
  - Feeding: algae-disk piece size, time added, time leftovers removed
  - Water changes (volume and time)
  - Microscope video clip (10–30 s) with clip number, saved as DISH_YYYYMMDD_HHMM_CLIP##.mp4
- **C1 and C2 only (planned):** pre-launch counts and activity; flight data from payload loggers (altitude, pressure, temperature, UV, radiation); after recovery, time to revival, survival rate, and reproduction over following weeks.
- **A:** routine husbandry logging on Form F1 (counts, water, feeding, subculture events).
