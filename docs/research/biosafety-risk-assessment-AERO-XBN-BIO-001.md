## 1\. Organism

  * Non-pathogenic, freshwater micro-animal sold by an educational supplier for classroom use.
  * Not known to infect or harm people, animals or plants. No genetic modification. Not a regulated agent.
  * Risk group: lowest. Work is done with Biosafety Level 1 (BSL-1) practices, the level used for school and teaching labs.



## 2\. Hazard and control table

Hazard| Likelihood| Severity| Controls  
---|---|---|---  
Skin or eye contact with culture water| Low| Low| Gloves and eye protection when handling; wash hands after every session.  
Spill of culture water| Low| Low| Spill steps in section 4; dishes kept on a tray.  
Release to the environment| Low| Low| No pouring live cultures into drains or outdoors; disposal per section 5.  
Broken glass / sharps (slides, coverslips)| Low| Moderate| Use plastic Petri dishes where possible; broken glass into a sharps or rigid container.  
Payload dish leak during flight| Low| Low| Sealed, double-contained payload (section 6).  
Microscope lamp heat / electrical| Low| Low| Lamp off when not viewing; cords inspected weekly; equipment log.  
  
## 3\. Handling practices (BSL-1)

  * No eating, drinking or applying cosmetics in the work area.
  * Wear gloves; wash hands with soap before and after handling.
  * Clean work surfaces before and after each session with 70% alcohol or a 10% bleach solution.
  * Use droppers and pipettes only for culture work; no mouth pipetting.
  * Label every container with group ID (AERO-554-A, B1, B2, C1, C2), culture ID 31585554 and date.



## 4\. Spills

  * Cover the spill with paper towel, apply 10% bleach solution, wait 10 minutes, wipe up and bag the towels.
  * Clean the area again with 70% alcohol. Record the spill on a Deviation / Incident Report (AERO-XBN-DEV-001).



## 5\. Disposal

  * Before disposal, treat culture water and organisms with 10% bleach for at least 30 minutes, or freeze, then pour to the sanitary drain with running water.
  * Used gloves, towels and plastic dishes go to regular trash in a closed bag after treatment.
  * Never release live cultures outdoors.



## 6\. Flight payload containment

  * Each flight dish (C1, C2) will be sealed (lid taped or screw-cap container), then placed in a second sealed, padded container inside the payload.
  * Containers will be checked for leaks before launch and at recovery (Pre-/Post-flight Checklists, AERO-XBN-CHK-001).
  * On recovery, sealed containers will be returned to the lab unopened and opened only on the work tray.
  * Payload and landing-site cleanup: any leaked material treated as a spill (section 4).



## 7\. Training and review

  * All handlers will be briefed on this assessment and SOP AERO-XBN-SOP-2026-001 by the experienced STEM team before handling.
  * This assessment is reviewed before each flight and whenever the procedure changes.
