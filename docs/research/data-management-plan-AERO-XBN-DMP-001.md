# Data Management Plan

AERO-XBN-DMP-001 Rev. A · Non-Human Biologics in the Stratosphere: A Study by Aerostratospheric

Culture ID / Lot:31585554

Received date:October 28, 2026

SOP:AERO-XBN-SOP-2026-001

## 1\. Purpose

  * Describes how study records for culture 31585554 (groups AERO-554-A, B1, B2, C1, C2) will be collected, named, stored, checked, shared and kept.
  * Applies to paper forms F1, F2 and all documents in this set, microscope video clips, payload logger files and ground environmental readings from the A.E.R.B. sensors.



## 2\. Data types

  * **Paper records:** F1 ground logs, F2 flight records, chain-of-custody, checklists, deviation reports, weekly summaries. Scanned or photographed weekly to PDF.
  * **Counts:** active, sluggish, tun, dead and eggs per dish, entered into a spreadsheet (CSV) from the paper sheets.
  * **Video:** 10 to 30 second microscope clips (MP4) per dish at each weekly count.
  * **Flight data:** payload logger files (altitude, pressure, temperature, UV, radiation, GPS track) as exported CSV plus the original file.
  * **Ground environment:** solar, UV, radiation and weather readings for launch and lab days.



## 3\. File naming

  * Sheets: FORM_DISH_YYYYMMDD.pdf, e.g. F1_AERO-554-B1_20261105.pdf.
  * Clips: DISH_YYYYMMDD_HHMM_CLIP##.mp4 (times CST), matching the Clip # written on the sheet.
  * Flight files: FLIGHTID_LOGGER_YYYYMMDD.csv; originals are never edited.
  * Weekly folders: YYYY-Www (ISO week).



## 4\. Storage and backup

  * Working copy in the private repository Midwest-Stratospheric/non-human-biologics-data (text, CSV, PDF) with large video in the research file store, organized by week.
  * Original paper sheets kept in a binder in the lab; nothing is discarded.
  * At least two copies at all times: the repository plus one offline backup, refreshed weekly.



## 5\. Quality checks

  * Each week, a second team member (JH, LA or OT) compares transcribed counts against the paper sheets and signs the Weekly Lab Notebook Summary.
  * Corrections follow the single-line, initial-and-date rule; transcription fixes are recorded in the repository history.
  * Missing or unusual entries are logged on a Deviation / Incident Report (AERO-XBN-DEV-001).



## 6\. Sharing and open data

  * Cleaned counts, scanned sheets, selected clips and flight logger data will be published through the Aerostratospheric Data Hub after team review.
  * License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0), as set out in AERO-XBN-DUA-001.
  * No personal information about volunteers or students is published; researcher codes (JH, LA, OT) are used in records.
  * Publication of the repository waits on the team's privacy and legal review.



## 7\. Retention

  * All raw records kept for at least 5 years after the last flight in the study.
  * Superseded documents are marked SUPERSEDED and kept, never deleted.



## 8\. Roles

  * Study lead: JH. Records and data entry: JH, LA, OT as assigned. Review: a team member other than the one who recorded the data.
